Who We Are

Indigenous. Intelligent. Impact-Driven.

We contribute to the energy value chain through renewable energy integration — on-grid, off-grid, and community mini-grid systems — engineered and delivered by Nigerians, for Nigeria and Africa.

Why Choose Us

Not Just Another Solar Company

There are many solar installers in Nigeria. There is only one Energy Metrics Africa. Here is what makes us genuinely different.

We measure your load first

Most installers guess. We profile your actual consumption before we touch a single component. No oversized invoices. No undersized batteries leaving you in the dark.

Only LiFePO4 batteries

4,000–6,000 charge cycles and a 10–15 year lifespan. We use it because it’s the only chemistry we trust to power the communities we serve.

Live monitoring, always

Most Nigerian solar owners get no post-installation support. We monitor every system in real time, you’ll know it’s working before you notice it isn’t.

Engineering documentation

Formal load calculations, single-line diagrams, and as-built documentation on every project, built to IEC 60364 because proper engineering leaves a paper trail.

Built for Nigerian realities

We understand power schedules, local supply chains, and FX volatility because we operate in the same environment you do.

The numbers, before you commit

Cost savings, payback period, and system lifespan calculated against your actual load. If the economics don’t work, we’ll tell you.
