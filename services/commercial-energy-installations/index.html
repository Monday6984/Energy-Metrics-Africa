What Unreliable Power Really Costs You

Hospitals

Nine hours of blackout. Theatres running on diesel. Money burned on a problem that shouldn’t exist.

Hotels

Lights flicker at dinner. That’s not only a bad night, but it’s also a guest who may never book again.

Factories

The line stops. You pay workers to wait while orders fall behind and clients start asking questions.

Offices

The servers, the AC, the lift — all dead, with a client watching. That’s not an inconvenience. It’s your credibility on the line.

The African Development Bank puts the cost of outages at 3% of annual sales for Nigerian businesses. Work out 3% of yours. Then add fuel, maintenance, and the generator you’ll replace.

Our Difference

Four things that separate a commercial installation.

01

We audit your full operational load

We look at the details that actually affect your power needs: peak production hours, overnight demand, seasonal changes and equipment startup surges. Then we design around your worst-case week, not your average day.

02

We work out the numbers before we propose anything

We start with what you spend today, what you are likely to spend after installation, when you reach payback, and what the system could cost you over 10 years compared with staying with your current setup. If the numbers do not make sense, we will tell you before you spend a naira.

03

We engineer and document the system

Single-line diagrams, protection device schedules, cable sizing and as-built drawings, plus relevant IEC 60364 compliance documentation. So when your insurer, facilities team or a future investor needs the paperwork, you have it.

04

We monitor the system

Once the system is running, we monitor it in real time. We track generation, battery state and faults as they happen. You do not have to sit around checking the system — we keep an eye on it for you.
