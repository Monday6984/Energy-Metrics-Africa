Our Difference

Four things we do that most installers skip.

01

We measure your actual load first

We profile every appliance, every hour of use, and your peak draw. Then we design for your home, not an average one. This is why your system still runs at midnight instead of going flat at 9 pm like your neighbour’s.

02

We show you the numbers before you spend anything

You see your projected monthly savings, payback period, and system lifespan before we order a single component. Calculated against your real usage. If the numbers do not work for you, we say so. We would rather lose a sale than install an unreliable system.

03

We build to IEC 60364.

The international standard for electrical installations. Formal load calculations, single line diagrams, cable sizing, protection devices. NEMSA has documented house fires from installations that ignored this. Every EMA system comes with full engineering documentation.

04

We monitor your system every day

Most Nigerian solar owners get zero support after installation. Their systems degrade quietly, and they have no idea until something fails. We connect every system to our monitoring platform. If output drops by more than 8% without a weather explanation, we flag it before you notice.

FAQs

The questions serious buyers have asked us.

In most cases, the battery is the problem. The wrong battery chemistry can degrade quickly in Nigeria’s heat. That’s why we use LiFePO4 batteries exclusively. They are rated for 4,000 to 6,000 full charge cycles. At one full cycle a day, that works out to roughly 11 to 16 years of use.

We don’t just install the system and leave. Every system is monitored from day one. When we hand it over, you get access to the monitoring app, and our team keeps an eye on its performance with you. If something changes, we know about it early.

There is no one-size-fits-all price because every home has different energy needs. If you currently spend between ₦80,000 and ₦150,000 a month on fuel and electricity, a properly sized system can typically pay for itself within 24 to 36 months. After that, you can significantly reduce your electricity and fuel costs for years to come.
