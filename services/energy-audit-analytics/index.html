The Diagnosis

Your bill tells you what you spent. Not why.

Most Nigerian homes and businesses have no idea where their energy spend goes. They reduce hours and remind staff to switch things off, yet the bill barely moves. That is because the waste is not where they are looking.

We put our eyes on every circuit.

Then we tell you exactly what to fix.

We install IoT sensors across your facility. Within 14 days, we have a complete picture of your consumption: by circuit, by hour, and by equipment. We identify every source of waste, calculate the cost of each one, and give you a ranked action list.

Average Saving 15 to 35% of monthly energy spend, with no core equipment replaced.
What We Measure

Six Things We Actually Check.

Equipment-Level Consumption

Every major load profiled individually, not estimated.

Peak Demand Patterns

The hours and equipment causing your highest tariff charges.

Power Quality

Voltage fluctuations, harmonics, and power factor issues costing you money silently.

Idle & Standby Waste

The equipment drawing load when your facility is closed.

Tariff Optimisation

Whether your current tariff band matches your actual usage pattern.

Billing Accuracy

Whether what you are being charged matches what you are actually consuming.

How It Works

Assessment to savings report in 30 days.

01

Site assessment

We map your facility and identify the major loads. It takes one day and does not disrupt your operations.

02

Sensor installation

We install IoT meters across your panels, circuits and major equipment. This usually takes one to two days, and your operation continues as normal.

03

14 days of data

We collect live consumption data across the facility for 14 days — a clear picture of what each circuit is using, when it is using it, and where your peak and off-peak consumption sits.

04

Analysis

Our engineers go through the data to find where energy is being wasted, then put a monthly cost on each issue so you can see what those inefficiencies are actually costing you.

05

Savings report

A ranked list of the opportunities we found. For each one, what it will cost to fix, how much you could save each month, and how long it should take to pay for itself. No jargon, just numbers you can use.

06

Implementation support

We stay involved while the changes are being made. You will know exactly what needs to change, what work is required, and what the expected result should be.
